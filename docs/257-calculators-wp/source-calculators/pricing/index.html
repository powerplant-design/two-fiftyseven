    FAQ

    Procurement answers, plainly.

    At two/fiftyseven, monthly memberships start at $109 for one day a week (Flexi 1) and run up to $659 for a permanent desk seven days a week (Dedicated). All prices ex GST. Day passes start at $40 single, or as low as $28 per day in a 50-pack. Comparable Wellington coworking sits at $450 to $650 per month for flexible memberships and $700 to $830 for dedicated desks.

    Desk access (Dedicated or Flexi), all meeting rooms, kitchen, fast internet, power, cleaning, accessible facilities, a Mental Health First Aid trained team on site, the 257 booking app, and access to community events. There are no fit-out costs, no utility bills, no surprise surcharges. Pricing is published; everyone pays the same rate.

    Dedicated ($659/mo) is your own permanent desk, seven days a week. Leave your gear, set up a second monitor, walk in to the same desk every day. Flexi (1 to 5 days a week, $109 to $509/mo) is a hot desk on the days you’re in. Same access to meeting rooms, kitchen, internet, and everything else.

    No. Month-to-month, no minimum term. Your membership continues until you give notice. We don’t require fixed-term contracts like traditional office leases or some peer coworking providers in Wellington.

    Yes. $40 for a single day pass, $350 for a 10-pack ($35/day), $650 for a 20-pack ($32.50/day), and $1,400 for a 50-pack ($28/day). All weekday-valid. Useful if you’re visiting Wellington, between roles, or want to test the workspace before committing to a monthly membership.

    Workspace support for charities, NGOs, indigenous-led orgs, community groups and social enterprises is need-based, not a fixed percentage. Tell us about your work and your situation, and we’ll figure out what fits. Email kiaora@twofiftyseven.co.

    Our Impact Discount of 50% off applies separately to meeting rooms and events — not to memberships. See the Impact page for the full criteria.

    All prices on this page are ex GST. NZ GST is currently 15%. Monthly invoices, standard payment terms.

    Each team member picks their own tier (Dedicated 7 days/week, or Flexi 5/4/3/2/1 days a week). The team total is the sum of those per-member rates. Use the proposal builder above to configure a mixed team, for example 2 Dedicated + 3 Flexi 3, and see the live monthly and annual total.

    Yes. Pay annually upfront and save 10% on the Dedicated tier ($7,116/yr vs $7,908/yr). Tick “Pay annually” in the proposal builder to see the discount applied to your live figure. Flexi tiers are billed monthly only with no annual prepay discount.

    Wellington’s flexible-membership coworking sits at $450 to $650 per person per month (low end: The Exchange Te Aro; high end: The Space Wellington, Urban Hub). Dedicated desks sit $700 to $830 (e.g. The Hangar, Icehouse, WOTSO, Thinkspace). two/fiftyseven Flexi starts at $109/mo for one day a week up to $509/mo for five days; Dedicated is $659/mo. Regus is excluded from the benchmark because its 24-month lock-in isn’t comparable flexibility.

    The private-office benchmark is $14,200 per person per year. It’s modelled on a central-Wellington B-grade private office and covers rent, outgoings, fit-out amortised over five years, internet, power, cleaning, kitchen and bathroom consumables, insurance, MHFA training, admin time, and lease legals. Your saving is that benchmark times your team size minus your configured two/fiftyseven total.
