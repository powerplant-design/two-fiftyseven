How the math works

What we measure. What we offset. What's separate.

We use Tadpole ACE 2025[1] · the NZ-standard carbon accounting tool, aligned with the MfE 2025 Measuring Emissions Guide[2]. The four steps below are the story your sustainability lead can follow line-by-line.

The four-step story

  1. Measurement

    Annual operational emissions measured against Tadpole ACE 2025 emission factors: 0.1011 + 0.0077 (line losses) = 0.1088 kgCO₂e/kWh for grid electricity; 0.584 kgCO₂e/kg for office waste to landfill with gas recovery; 0.4797 kgCO₂e/day for WFH equivalent. Independently calculated; numbers below.

  2. Renewable supply

    Electricity supplied by Ecotricity at 125% Toitū climate positive certification[3]. Ecotricity is NZ's only Toitū climate positive certified energy product, offsetting 125% of cradle-to-grave emissions for every unit supplied · meaning the electricity that powers two/fiftyseven is already climate-positive at supply, before any further intervention on our side.

  3. Carbon credits

    We purchase 24 Ekos NZUs annually[4] against the residual measured footprint. Combined with the Ecotricity 125% supply, the total offset position lands at approximately 200% of our measured emissions · twice what we emit, publicly claimed and externally verifiable.

  4. Biodiversity credits · a separate stream

    We also fund biodiversity restoration at Sanctuary Mountain Maungatautari (3,363 ha) via Ekos BioCredita[5]. Per Ekos founder Sean Weaver, "Ekos biodiversity credits are not offsets of any kind. The Ekos BioCredita programme does not commodify nature or put a price on nature. Instead, it puts a price on the human labour and technology cost to look after nature · just like philanthropy does." We list this stream separately and never aggregate it into the carbon offset percentage.

Public-facing claim: 200% offset. The internal math runs to approximately 238%, but we round our public claim conservatively to 200%. This protects against year-on-year variance, methodology updates, and the risk that an auditor reaches a slightly different number from the same inputs.

What's excluded · and why

The current footprint covers operational scope 1 + 2 + the most material scope 3 items (waste, water, contractor commute). It does not currently include: embodied carbon in fit-out and furniture, member commute (we collect it through a voluntary annual survey but don't price it into the calc), upstream value chain emissions of our suppliers, food and beverage scope 3, or staff business travel.

Phase 2.3 of this calculator will surface honest estimates for embodied fit-out carbon and member commute as separate display lines · flagged as estimates, not measured. The brief says "honest about scope" and that means flagging the gap rather than papering over it.

Review cadence + disclaimer

Reviewed annually after our FY footprint is locked, and within four weeks of: a new MfE Measuring Emissions Guide release, a Tadpole ACE annual factor update, an Ecotricity certification status change, or an Ekos NZU portfolio update. Last reviewed May 2026. Next scheduled review: May 2027.
