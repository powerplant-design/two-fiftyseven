How the math works

where the Wellington pricing comes from.

Local room pricing band

The "current local pricing" figure is sampled from five comparable central-Wellington venues' published 2026 rate cards. We take the mid of that sample, not the low or high — the calc compares against a realistic middle-of-market price, not a best- or worst-case quote. Source: Wellington City Council venue hire1, The Boatshed Wellington2, Te Wharewaka o Pōneke3, Spaces NZ Wellington4, Tory Street Co-op5.

Catering per head

Local catering rates are sampled from three operators we work with (and many teams independently engage). Per-head rates for tea & coffee, light breakfast, lunch boxes, hot lunches, and evening drinks are taken from each operator's 2026 published menu, mid of the range. Source: Karaka Cafe (Māori kai)6, Blue Carrot (plant-led)7, Food Envy (full-service)8.

AV + technician time

Projector + sound add-ons priced at Wellington event-industry standard hire rate ($50–$120/day depending on spec). Technician time at $75–$125/hour per NZ AV industry 2026 benchmark. Source: Production-Design NZ9, NZ Event Production Industry pricing benchmark10.

two/fiftyseven figure

Our number is our own published price for the same room + spec, applied identically to the same scenario. The Impact Discount toggle cuts that figure by 50% (rooms only, not catering or AV). Source: published rates at meetings/pricing/, current as of 2026.

FAQ

Common questions

A small Wellington meeting room (4–8 people, half-day) typically runs $350–$650 at city-centre venues; a full-day workshop room for 20 people lands around $850–$1,900 once room hire, tea + coffee and a light lunch are in. At two/fiftyseven the same 20-person full-day workshop starts at $690 for the room, with tea + coffee at $5/head and catering free when you arrange it directly, charged at cost when we arrange it.

For 20 people, the typical Wellington workshop-venue full-day spend is $1,500–$3,000 once you add room hire, catering, AV and materials. The calculator above gives you the exact line items · and shows the equivalent build at two/fiftyseven, where the workshop space is $690/day, tea + coffee is included, and AV is offered as small separate add-ons at maintenance-replacement cost so teams not using AV aren't paying for it.

For a small team (under 12), our Silver Linings room at $590/day or Studios Whātaitai + Ngake at $490/day with tea + coffee included tend to be the lowest credible spend. Add catering at cost from approved local suppliers and you can run a full-day away day for 10 people from around $700–$900 all-in.

Room hire includes tea + coffee, fast wifi, whiteboards and flipcharts, hosting by an MHFR + First Aid-trained team, and a climate-positive venue with 200% verified offset. Projector + sound system are separate small add-ons at maintenance-replacement cost · accessible for teams who don't need them. Catering is free when you arrange it directly, charged at cost when we arrange it.
