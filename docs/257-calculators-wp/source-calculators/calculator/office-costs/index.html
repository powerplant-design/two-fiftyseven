    Further considerations

    Real costs the calculator above doesn't model

    • Lease term + CPI escalation

      Standard NZ commercial term is 3 years; rent typically escalates 2·4% per year tied to CPI or a fixed schedule. Multi-year cashflow modelling needs this on top of the calculator's year-1 base.

    • End-of-lease make-good

      Most ADLS leases require return-to-original-condition · paint, carpet, partition removal, plant reinstatement. Budget 1·3 months of rent equivalent as a make-good reserve.

    • Lease incentives

      Landlord-side concessions: rent-free periods of 1·6 months; fit-out contributions of $300·$800/m². These offset year-1 cost but aren't standard on every deal · negotiate.

    • Bank guarantee + bond

      Most landlords require a 3-month bank guarantee on top of any cash bond. The guarantee ties up working capital and carries its own bank fee.

    • Personal guarantee

      Sole traders + small businesses are routinely asked for personal guarantees. This is a real risk · default on the lease and the landlord can pursue you personally for the remaining term.

    • Tenant vs landlord insurance overlap

      Public liability, contents and business interruption typically sit with the tenant; building insurance with the landlord (recovered via outgoings). Confirm the split with your broker before relying on the calculator's insurance line.

    • Capex on top of furniture

      Out of scope above: phone system, IT cabling install, signage, partitions + walls, kitchen install, AV. Budget separately if your fit-out scope extends beyond standard amortised furniture.
