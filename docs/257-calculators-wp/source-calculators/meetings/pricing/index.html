FAQ

Before you book a meeting room.

Meeting rooms at two/fiftyseven start at $70/hr or $290/day for the six-person Meeting Room. Silver Linings (12) is $140/hr or $690/day. The Studio (16) is $110/hr or $590/day. The Workshop Space (40) is $180/hr or $790/day. The Event Space (100) is $280/hr or $1,190/day. The whole venue (200) is $380/hr or $1,900/day. All prices ex GST. Charities, NGOs, indigenous-led organisations, community groups and social enterprises book at up to 50% off under the Impact Discount.

The room set to your run-sheet before you arrive, tea, coffee and water on arrival, fast business-grade wifi, AV (screens, projector, mics, dongles, wireless screen-share, checked before you arrive in the rooms that have it), full-wall whiteboards in the rooms that have them, and pack-down by us. Catering runs through Karaka Cafe, Blue Carrot or Food Envy and is passed through at cost, no markup. Lift access from both Willis Street and Victoria Street.

The Impact Discount is up to 50% off room hire for: charities, NGOs, B-Corps and not-for-profits with annual revenue under $200k; certified carbon-zero or carbon-positive organisations under $200k revenue; community-based volunteer organisations; and tangata whenua or indigenous-led organisations. The discount applies to room hire only, not catering or add-ons. If you're not sure where you fit, email kiaora@twofiftyseven.co with a one-line description and we confirm within a working day.

All prices on this page are ex GST. NZ GST is currently 15%. Your invoice will show the GST line separately so it's clear for your finance team.

Catering runs through our three local partners — Karaka Cafe (Māori kai), Blue Carrot (plant-led platters and lunches) and Food Envy (full-service hot meals). All three know our kitchen setup and the way meetings here run. Catering is passed through at cost — you set your own per-head budget and we add a $100 organising fee. Or organise your own catering from any other provider; you're absolutely welcome to.

For meetings, a few business days is comfortable; same-day is often possible for the Meeting Room and the Studio. For workshops and away days, two to four weeks lead time gives us room to configure the day around your run-sheet. For the Event Space or a whole-venue booking, four to six weeks is sensible. Email kiaora@twofiftyseven.co and we reply within one working day.

A meeting is short and focused — boardroom, video calls, briefings — typically 1 to 4 hours in the Meeting Room, Silver Linings or a Studio. A workshop is session-focused, usually 3 to 4 hours with breakouts, often externally facilitated — usually in the Workshop Space or a Studio. An away day is a full-day team off-site, typically 09:00–17:00 with shared meals — usually in the Workshop Space configured for the day. Same rooms, different shapes.

Yes. Multiple rooms work well when you want a plenary plus breakouts — typically the Workshop Space or Event Space alongside a Studio for splinter sessions. The whole-venue rate ($380/hr or $1,900/day) gives you every room for the day, including the workspace, and is the option most events of 100+ people take.
