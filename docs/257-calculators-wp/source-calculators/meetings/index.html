Set-up and pack-down

Tea + coffee

Organic, brewed fresh and in your room when you arrive.

Wifi

Fast business-grade wifi on every device, every room.

FAQ

Before you book.

The Meeting Room — a sound-isolated six-person room with a full-wall whiteboard, screen, wireless screen-share, fast wifi and tea/coffee on the table. Booked by the hour or the day: $70/hr or $290/day. Best for confidential discussions, focused video calls, and one-on-ones that need privacy.

Two options. Silver Linings is a twelve-person boardroom with natural light and high-quality video conferencing — $120/hr, $360/evening, $590/day — best when the room itself needs to take the meeting seriously. Studios Whātaitai + Ngake are slightly more relaxed sixteen-person studios at $90/hr, $290/evening, $490/day per studio — book one studio or both as two separate rooms.

Yes. The Event Space is built for evening hire · 150 people, full AV, optional bar service, Victoria Street entry that runs into the evening opposite Matapihi ki te Ao Nui. DIY rental from $280/hr or $1,190/day. Silver Linings and the studios also book by the evening for smaller after-hours functions: Silver Linings at $360/evening (4-hour block), studios at $290/evening per studio.

All prices. Charities, NGOs and indigenous-led organisations book at up to 50% off under the Impact Discount.

Yes. Step-free entry from both Willis Street and Victoria Street, lift access to Level 2 from both, accessible bathrooms, non-gendered facilities, a shower on site. The whole space is on one level, with wide pathways between rooms. We can pre-arrange seating, hearing-loop equipment and a quiet breakout room — flag anything we should set up at booking.

Both. We work with three local catering partners — Karaka Cafe (Māori kai), Blue Carrot (plant-led platters) and Food Envy (full-service hot meals and canapés) — at no markup, no minimum, ordered through us. Or you can BYO. For evening events the bar is run by our team, or you can BYO that too.

Charities, NGOs, indigenous-led organisations, community groups and social enterprises doing for-purpose work in Aotearoa book any meeting room at up to 50% off — same room, same access, different invoice. The discount is offered automatically once we've confirmed eligibility; no separate application form. It applies to room hire only, not workspace memberships. Every hour booked here funds about $1 of subsidised space for another for-purpose org.

Email kiaora@twofiftyseven.co with the day, the room you have in mind (or what you're running and we'll suggest one), rough shape, number of people and any catering needs. We send a written quote within a working day and hold the room while you decide. Same-day bookings answered within an hour during business hours (08:00–18:00 weekdays).
